About Me

I am a B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, with a strong interest in Generative AI, Machine Learning, Python, NLP, and Data Analytics. I enjoy building practical AI-driven applications that transform data and information into useful, real-world solutions.

My technical foundation includes Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Generative AI, LLMs, NLP, Pandas, EDA, Tableau, LangChain, FastAPI, Git, and GitHub. I focus on applying these technologies through hands-on projects rather than learning them only theoretically.

One of my key projects, TeamTrack Pro, is a full-stack AI/ML platform for project management and team analytics. It includes AI-powered skill extraction from task logs, candidate recommendation using Machine Learning and Logistic Regression, an ATS-friendly resume generator, and an AI career assistant for skill-gap analysis.

I also built EduGen AI, a Generative AI application that converts YouTube lectures into structured study material. The project uses Python, LLMs, Generative AI, and Hugging Face Whisper for transcript extraction, audio transcription when captions are unavailable, transcript processing, and generation of concise, exam-ready study material.

During my Data Engineering internship at Decodelabs, I worked with Python, Pandas, and Tableau to build a data preprocessing pipeline involving data cleaning, missing-value handling, categorical-data standardization, and preparation of datasets for analysis and visualization.

Alongside my technical work, I am continuously strengthening my understanding of Object-Oriented Programming and Machine Learning while expanding my practical knowledge of AI and data-driven application development.

Currently, I am looking for entry-level opportunities and internships in Generative AI, AI/ML, Machine Learning, Python Development, Data Analytics, Data Science, and related technology roles, where I can apply my skills, learn from experienced teams, and contribute to meaningful projects.

I am always open to learning, collaborating, building, and connecting with people working in AI, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Python, and Data.
